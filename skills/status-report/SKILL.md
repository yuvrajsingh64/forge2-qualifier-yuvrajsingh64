---
name: status-report
description: Post a structured progress update to Slack channel #sprint-main.
trigger: when asked for a status update, progress report, or what's been done
---

Gather:
1. Tasks completed since last update
2. Tasks still in progress or pending
3. Decisions or blockers that need the human

Reply in exactly three sections:

**What I Did**
[completed tasks as bullet points]

**What's Left**
[remaining tasks with rough effort]

**What Needs Your Call**
[decisions, blockers, or approvals needed]

Post to #sprint-main. Keep each section to three to five bullets.
